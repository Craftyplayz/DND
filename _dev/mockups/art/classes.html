Class icons: pick a style

All 20 classes in three styles, drawn from scratch. Each is shown at 40 px and 80 px on a white row and on the site's dark background; the cards at the end of each row mimic the builder list. Pick A, B or C per class, tick redo if none work.

A Tile emblem: coloured rounded square, flat white emblem.
B Engraved medallion: dark coin, fine metallic line-art, double ring.
C Bold glyph: one colour, no background, built for small sizes.
